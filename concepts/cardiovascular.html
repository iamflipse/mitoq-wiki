MitoQ & Cardiovascular Function

Source: §9.1 of the paper

Why mitochondria matter for vascular aging

  • The endothelium produces NO via eNOS; aging → ↓NO bioavailability from increased oxidative stress = independent CVD risk factor.
  • Superoxide (O₂⁻) from dysfunctional mitochondria reacts with NO → peroxynitrite (ONOO⁻), destroying NO bioavailability and impairing endothelium-dependent vasodilation.
  • Superoxide also causes eNOS uncoupling and oxidizes the cofactor BH4 → impaired NO production.

Preclinical (aged rodent models)

  • MitoQ (acute or 4-week long-term) restores endothelium-dependent dilation (EDD) in carotid artery by improving NO bioavailability (Fig. 3).
  • Mechanisms: ↓mtROS scavenging of NO; ↓p66Shc (redox-sensing protein whose expression rises with age) limiting ROS accumulation; ↑COX-IV (mitochondrial biogenesis marker) → improved mitochondrial function.
  • Chronic MitoQ in aged mice: mitigates age-related ↓elastin expression / elastin area fraction → improved arterial compliance/stiffness.

Human vascular evidence

  • Isolated human skeletal-muscle feed arteries + MitoQ: improved EDD, ↑NO bioavailability, eNOS activation, ↓mitochondrial oxidative stress; tied to preserved mitochondrial state III respiration.
  • Single-dose MitoQ improved FMD in a cardiorespiratory fitness study; also in postmenopausal women with late-onset menopause.
  • 6-week supplementation in older adults: ↓circulating oxLDL (oxidized LDL), accompanied by ↓mtROS bioactivity and ↑NO production.
  • CKD stage 3–4: 4-week oral MitoQ (20 mg/day) improves conduit-artery FMD + microvascular conductance via NADPH oxidase ablation.
  • PAD (Fontaine II–III): single 80 mg oral dose ↑brachial/popliteal FMD + plasma SOD; extends max walking time/distance and claudication onset time.

Pressure-overload heart failure

  • AAC mouse model: 1 week of MitoQ improves FS/EF, normalizes LV dimensions; redox-sensitive Plscr4–miR-214 axis normalized → preserves MFN2 (mitochondrial network integrity, membrane potential) and enhances mitochondria–SR colocalization.
  • TAC rat model (14 weeks): improves mitochondrial bioenergetics, normalizes TAC-induced H₂O₂, enhances mitochondrial Ca²⁺ retention, attenuates mPTP opening, ↑state III respiration, RCR, fatty-acid oxidation.
  • Caveat: established LV systolic dysfunction not fully reversed; MitoQ reduced RV hypertrophy and lung congestion — a disease-modifying rather than curative effect.

# MitoQ & Age-Related Physiological Dysfunction — Wiki

> A curated wiki distilling the 2026 *GeroScience* (Springer) review:
> **Therapeutic potential of MitoQ, a mitochondria-targeted antioxidant, in age-related physiological dysfunction** (Kim et al., GeroScience 2026; DOI 10.1007/s11357-026-02515-1).

The paper reviews how **mitochondrial oxidative stress** drives age-related dysfunction, and evaluates **MitoQ** — a ubiquinone conjugated to a lipophilic triphenylphosphonium (TPP⁺) cation that selectively accumulates in mitochondria — across the **cardiovascular, neuronal, skeletal muscle, and lower urinary tract** systems.

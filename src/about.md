*GeroScience*, 2026. DOI: [10.1007/s11357-026-02515-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-026-02515-1).

## Core thesis

Aging increases mitochondrial oxidative stress (mtROS). MitoQ — ubiquinone + TPP⁺ — concentrates in mitochondria via the membrane potential and scavenges mtROS. The review catalogs MitoQ's organ-specific effects: **cardiovascular** (NO bioavailability, oxLDL, heart failure), **neuronal** (ONOO⁻, memory, AD), **skeletal muscle** (sarcopenia — results are dose/duration-dependent), and **lower urinary tract** (LUTS linked to metabolic syndrome and NLRP3).
